# Phase 8 — Legal and commercial approvals

**These are requirements for counsel and the owner. They are not legal documents and they are not approved.**

Counsel should review, and the owner should not publish, drafts of:

| Document | Must cover | Must not claim |
|----------|------------|----------------|
| Terms of Service | Who may use the source and any future hosted service | A certification or an uptime warranty |
| Privacy Policy | Account data, agent prompts if stored, evidence, subprocessors actually used | Processors that are not contracted |
| Data Processing Agreement | Customer roles, instructions, subprocessors, deletion | A signed DPA that does not exist |
| Retention and deletion | What leaves the system on cancel, and when | A retention period engineering invented |
| Subprocessor list | Hetzner, Cloudflare, and a payment provider only if contracted | Google Cloud unless the owner funds it |
| Support terms | Channel and severity response the owner will staff | The proposed RTO/RPO as a contract |
| Commercial terms | Plans the owner approved | Prices, trials, or refunds not in `PHASE_07_COMMERCIAL_OWNER_DECISIONS.md` |

Existing files `TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md` and `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` are repository documents. This pass does not represent them as counsel-approved for a hosted launch.
