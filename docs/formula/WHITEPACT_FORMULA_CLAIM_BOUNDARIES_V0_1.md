# WhitePact Formula — Claim Boundaries v0.1

Public, product, and research statements must stay inside these boundaries.

---

## Forbidden claims

| Claim | Why forbidden |
|-------|----------------|
| “WhitePact understands every AI system.” | Non-omniscience axiom; incomplete models |
| “WhitePact predicts every future.” | `F_H` is bounded, approximate |
| “WhitePact mathematically proves an AI is safe.” | No total safety theorem |
| “WhitePact controls all AI.” | Authority is external; Formula judges |
| “WhitePact Formula is universally complete.” | Graph/search bounds → INCOMPLETE |
| “Capability gravity / authority tensor” as science | Removed metaphors |
| “Formula EXECUTE grants permission.” | Grants are external events |

---

## Acceptable research framing

> WhitePact Formula is a model-independent calculus for computing bounded authority, reachable capability, causal consequence, and admissible execution states under explicit assumptions and bounded system models.

Qualify every demo with: versions, bounds, graph completeness, and UNKNOWN handling.

---

## Acceptable product positioning (non-theorem)

> WhitePact computes what an AI can cause before deciding what it may do.

This is **positioning**, not a formal guarantee. Pair with claim boundaries in sales/engineering docs.

---

## What v0.1 can honestly claim

- Precise definitions for `W_t`, nested sets, PO-*, and `J_Ω` outputs  
- Explicit separation of capability vs authority  
- Documented falsification criteria  
- Computational honesty (INCOMPLETE, UNKNOWN)  
- Alignment with WhitePact reconciliation doctrine for lost ack  

---

## What requires later gates + evidence

- Incident reduction  
- Production latency SLOs  
- Signed tag / release alignment (V1 process)  
- PyPI artifact attestation linkage to Formula proofs  

---

## Version discipline

Claims must cite `FormulaVersion`. v0.1 claims do not transfer to v0.2 without re-validation.
