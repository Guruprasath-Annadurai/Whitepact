Evaluate your operating boundary

Assess deployment, identity lifecycle, tenant scope and evidence requirements against the configuration you intend to operate.

Deployment is a responsibility

Define the supported integration paths and their trusted computing boundary. Review secrets, storage, transport, backup, recovery and isolation requirements for your environment. Repository implementation is not a claim that a hosted commercial service is available or production validated.

Identity and lifecycle

Map human and machine principals to explicit authority and organization scope. Evaluate provisioning, credential rotation, revocation and approval responsibilities against the current backend contract. A paid entitlement does not authorize agent action.

Tenant and data boundaries

Validate organization-scoped data access and the storage configuration with your own tenancy and retention requirements. Operators must verify deployment settings and access paths; a corporate page cannot establish tenant isolation for a particular installation.

Evidence and SIEM planning

Review the available evidence and incident records, their schemas and authenticated access controls against your audit workflow. Confirm the supported export or ingestion path before planning a SIEM integration. Hash-chain verification does not replace storage protection or external reconciliation.

Operational acceptance

Test denied, approval-required, expired or revoked authorization and UNKNOWN outcomes on each supported consequential path. Establish reconciliation procedures before retrying uncertain operations. Revocation cannot reverse a completed external effect.

Commercial and assurance review

Treat source implementation, deployed capability, commercial availability and external certification as separate acceptance criteria. Confirm configuration, responsibilities and any agreement directly; this page introduces no certification, staffed support or SLA commitment.
